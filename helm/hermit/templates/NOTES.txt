No tokens are stored in the chart: secrets.webhookSecret, secrets.gitRWToken,
secrets.gitReadToken (and optional secrets.vllmApiKey) each reference an
existing Kubernetes Secret by name and key (same namespace). Verify those
references resolve and hold:

- webhookSecret: matches the secret configured on your webhook,
- gitRWToken (write): lets the bot post reviews,
- gitReadToken (read-only): lets reviewer pods clone the repository,
- vllmApiKey (optional): forwarded to your vLLM endpoint by reviewer pods.

H.E.R.M.I.T only ever posts review comments; it never merges or commits.
